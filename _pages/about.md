I am currently a PhD candidate in computer science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, advised by [Josiah Hanna](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jphanna/index.html). My research is focused on enabling intelligent agents to perform complex tasks in the real world.
Toward this end, my research is focusing on meta-reasoning with reinforcement learning, i.e., how agent's can learn to allocate computation to perform tasks.
Currently, I'm working on meta-reasoning with RL in real-time, dynamic domains, VLAs for [workplace robotics](https://integrate.wisc.edu/) as an NSF NRT fellow, and I have worked on [RoboCup soccer](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jphanna/robocup.html) since I started my PhD, and currently help lead the team.
