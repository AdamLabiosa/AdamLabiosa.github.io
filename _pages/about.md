I am currently a PhD candidate in computer science at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/), advised by [Josiah Hanna](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jphanna/index.html). My research is focused on enabling intelligent agents to perform complex tasks in the real world. Toward this end, I study reinforcement learning (RL), a branch of machine learning where agent learn through interaction.

My dissertation is focused on meta-reasoning with RL in real-time domains, i.e., how can an agent learn to allocate computation. Additionally, I'm working on VLAs for [workplace robotics](https://integrate.wisc.edu/) as an NSF NRT fellow and [RoboCup soccer](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jphanna/robocup.html) on our RoboCup team.

Previously, I completed my BS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://engineering.wisc.edu/).
